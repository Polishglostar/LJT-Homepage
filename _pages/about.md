# About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.ust.hk/), advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://junxianhe.github.io/). My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML), with specific interests in:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
